**Who you are (in one line):** Dammie Optimus — QC staff at Starium Rafa, the developer who built the ERP.
**Your goal:** Introduce yourself, show what you built, and ask for his phone number + email so you can send the full demo materials.

> Tip: Send the DM first. Do NOT send the video links or the long email yet — get his contact details, then follow up properly. A short, confident DM gets a reply; a wall of text gets ignored.

## If He Says "Send It To My Staff/Managers Instead"

> "Of course, Sir — I'll share it with them as well. But I'd still appreciate having your personal contact, Sir, so I can keep you informed directly as the system grows. May I have your phone number or email?"

---

## Follow-Up Plan (if he doesn't reply in 3–4 days)

1. Reply to your own first message (a second message in the same thread — do NOT send a new DM):
   > "Good afternoon Sir, just following up on my earlier message — the factory system I built is complete and running live in my test environment, ready for deployment whenever you approve. I'd love to send you the demo videos. Happy to work around your schedule."
2. If still no reply after another week, mention it to his managers politely — they may have seen the demos already and know the system works.
3. Never beg, never spam. One confident follow-up is enough.

## The Direct Ask (memorize this exact sentence)

> **"Could I have your phone number or email address, Sir, so I can send you the demo videos?"**

That sentence is the whole point of the call. Say it clearly, then stop talking and wait for his answer.
