It is real-time, offline-first (it keeps working when factory internet drops), and runs in live test environments on three redundant hosting platforms, accessible from any phone, tablet, or laptop browser.

Because I work on the factory floor, the system was shaped around our actual processes — the QC inspector's routine, the shift handover, the warehouse workflow — not around assumptions made from outside. That is why staff can adopt it without friction.
