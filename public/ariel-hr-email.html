Track Record That Speaks for Itself

What began as a simple digital tool to make my own QC work easier — replacing paper-based powder density records with a digital form — grew into the Starium Rafa Enterprise ERP: a quality-control command suite, inspection and batch lineage, waste and yield intelligence, machine and silo health, pallet and stores ledger, and a governance layer with three-tier role-based access. It is real-time, offline-first (it keeps working when factory internet drops), and runs live on three redundant hosting platforms, accessible from any phone, tablet, or laptop browser.

Because I have worked on a factory floor since February, the system was shaped around real operational rhythm — the QC inspector's routine, the shift handover, the warehouse workflow — not around assumptions made from a desk. The same design discipline transfers directly to peanut intake, roasting and milling, blending with the 23 vitamins and minerals, lab QC, packing, warehousing and donor-facing reporting.
