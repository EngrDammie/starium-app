03Why Each Spec Matters Here
  • RAM is the ceiling. VS Code + Vite + Firebase emulators + two browsers + Docker exceeds 16GB today. 32GB means every tool stays open at once — critical when a deployment deadline or an urgent fix is on the line.
  • Gen 4 NVMe halves build times; node_modules, emulator images and SDKs devour hundreds of gigabytes within months. 512GB fills fast; 1TB buys years.
  • H-series CPUs sustain. Efficient U-chips throttle during hour-long build/test cycles; H-series hold boost clocks when a release is urgent.
  • 300+ nits matters under factory lighting — bright halls wash out dim screens during line-side demos and troubleshooting.
  • MIL-STD-810H is insurance against this exact environment: dust, vibration, heat, drops. Consumer laptops fail precisely there.
  • USB-C PD means power resilience: during outages the machine sips from a power bank or inverter — the hardware version of the offline-first philosophy I engineer by.
  • TPM 2.0 + biometrics protect company IP — the entire source code, credentials and operational data models of the ERP live on this device.
  • Spill-resistant keyboards exist because factories happen. One accident should not end the toolchain.
Critical buying rule — avoid soldered 16GB RAM configurations. If memory cannot be upgraded later, buy 32GB upfront. Soldered-RAM economy purchases become 18-month regrets; upgradeable ones become 5-year machines.

UK-Used Inspection Checklist (Before Paying)

  • Battery cycle count vs design capacity (powercfg /batteryreport)
  • All RAM slots present & populated — open the back panel, insist
  • SSD health via CrystalDiskInfo (power-on hours, wear level)
  • Dead pixels / backlight bleed on pure white & black screens
  • Hinge tension — loose hinges predict chassis failure
  • Every port tested live: USB, HDMI, audio, charging
  • Keyboard: every key pressed; trackpoint drift check
  • Serial number verified on Lenovo/HP/Dell warranty lookup
  • BIOS unlocked — supervisor passwords signal stolen units
  • Fan noise and throttling under a short stress test
  • Original charger included, correct wattage
  • Honest grade: A = minimal wear · B = cosmetic marks · C = walk away
