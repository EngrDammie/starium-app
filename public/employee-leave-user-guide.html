Employees & Leave
The Plain-English Guide

How to ask for leave. How it gets approved. How to add a new person. Everything is written here in everyday words, so nobody needs to ask anybody anything. If you can send a message on a phone, you can do all of this.

For: everyone at the factory Plain English Works on your phone Read time: ~8 minutes

01 The one-minute version

The paper leave form is now an app. You ask on your phone. The app counts your days. Three people sign. You watch it happen.

🚫 The old way

Find a form. Work out your own days with a pen. Walk it to three offices and hope. Come back later and ask: "has it been signed?" Nobody knows.

✅ The new way

Open My Leave. Pick your dates. The app does the counting — it knows the holidays. Press submit. Watch the three signatures happen, live, on your phone.

💡
The best part

The app and the printed form always agree — because the app makes both. No more hand-maths. No more "which copy is the real one?"

02 What you need before you start

Two things, and only two.

1

A login

HR gives you an email address and a password for the app. If you can sign in, you are ready. If you have no login yet, ask HR — it takes them one click to make you one.

2

Nothing else

No forms. No photocopies. Your days, your name, your department and your staff number are already in the system, filled in for you.

👤
If the app says "No employee record linked to this login"

Nothing is broken. It just means HR has not connected your login to your staff record yet. Ask HR. They will connect it, and your leave will appear.

03 My Leave — your one screen

Everything about your leave lives on one screen. Find it in the menu under Human Resources → My Leave.

The four numbers at the top

The word on screenWhat it means
EntitledThe days you may take this year. Joined halfway through the year? You get a fair share — not zero, and not the full year.
Already takenDays you have used this year. Only approved leave counts here.
Carried overDays you did not use last year, kept for you (up to 5 days).
AvailableWhat you have left, right now. This is the number you care about. Days still awaiting approval already count against it — the screen tells you whenever that applies.

Below the four numbers is a list of every request you have ever made, each with a coloured tag showing where it is. You will learn the tags in section 06.

🔍
These numbers are the same everywhere

The numbers on your screen, the ones HR sees, and the ones on the printed form all come from the same place. Nobody can see one number and somebody else see a different one.

04 How to ask for leave, step by step

Seven steps. About two minutes. On your phone, from anywhere.

1

Press "Request Leave"

The big button on your My Leave screen.

2

Pick your type of leave

Annual. Maternity. Casual. Compassionate. Examination. The form will ask you for a short reason on every type except annual — write one line. Maternity, compassionate and examination leave do not draw on your day balance; each has a maximum length that HR sets (currently 60, 5 and 10 working days).

3

Pick your first day and your last day

Two date boxes. That is the whole question.

4

Look at the boxes — the app has already counted

As soon as you pick dates, the app works out your days. You never count on your fingers. You never subtract a Sunday by mistake. Section 05 explains every box.

5

Say who is covering you

Type their name in "Handing over to". For leave of 3 working days or more, the app also asks for a short handover note: what should the next person watch out for?

6

Sign

Tick the box, then type your full name. That typing is your signature, with the date and time kept by the app.

7

Press "Submit Leave Request"

Done. The app tells you who has it now. You can go back to work.

⏰
One thing to plan: notice

Annual leave needs a week's notice. Emergencies — compassionate, sudden illness — do not. Pick dates that are too soon and the app tells you plainly, in a list right above the Submit button, exactly how many days short you are — and the button stays off until the list is empty.

07 Changing your mind

Plans change. Here is what you can undo, and when.

✅ While it still says "Awaiting…"

Press Cancel on the request. It is yours, it is not approved yet, and taking it back is one press. Nothing is lost — it stays on your list marked Cancelled, so the story stays honest.

🔒 After it says "Approved"

The leave has been signed for and the days are committed. Ask HR to change it — they are the only ones who can, and the change is recorded.

💬
If your leave was rejected

Open the request and read the reason — the approver had to write one. Speak to them, or to HR. If the dates were the problem, simply request again with different dates. Being rejected is not a black mark; it is usually about cover.

08 Coming back to work

One press on your first day back. That is the whole job.

When your leave is over, the app shows a friendly reminder: "You are due back." Press "Confirm my return" and your leave record closes itself.

📖
Why the app asks

The paper form had a box for "resumption of duty" that people forgot to fill. When you confirm, the record is complete — your counts are right, and HR does not have to chase you.

09 No internet? Read this

The factory Wi-Fi fails. The app was built knowing that.

✅ Asking for leave works offline

No signal? Ask anyway. The app saves your request safely on your phone and sends it the moment you have signal again. You will see: "Saved — waiting to send."

🛑 Signing does not work offline

On purpose. A signature says "this person's leave is approved and paid" — a decision like that must go straight into the real record, not sit on one phone. Approvers see a plain message and the buttons switch off until signal returns.

10 Questions people ask most

The eight questions that come up, answered once and for all.

Why doesn't Sunday count against me?
Rest days and public holidays are never deducted. HR decides which days are rest days, and if the factory works on Saturdays too, HR ticks that in the settings. Whatever they choose, the counting follows.
Why isn't my "Available" 21 days?
Three honest reasons, all shown on your screen: you may have joined mid-year (fair share, not the whole year), you may already have taken days, and last year's leftovers are capped at 5 days. Nothing is hidden.
My leave was rejected. What now?
Read the reason on the request — the approver had to write one. Talk to them or to HR. If the dates were the problem, ask again with different dates.
The app says I have no employee record.
Ask HR to add you to the roster and connect your login. Nothing is broken; you just are not linked yet. It takes HR a minute.
When exactly do I come back?
The "Resumption date" shown on your request — always the next working day after your leave ends. If your leave ends on a Friday, you are back the next working day, not the Saturday.
Can I book leave for dates I already have a request for?
No. One person cannot be booked twice for the same day. If your new dates touch a request you already have, the app tells you which one and what to do: cancel it yourself if it is still waiting, ask HR if it is already approved. Dates from a cancelled or rejected request can be booked again freely.
Can two of us be off at the same time?
Yes. When you pick dates, the app tells you who else is away then. If your department gets thin on cover it warns you — but people decide, not the app.
Who can see why I am on leave?
You, the people who sign your request, and HR. The dashboard everyone sees shows only names and dates — never reasons. A private matter stays private.
What if a public holiday falls inside my dates?
The app lists it under your dates and does not deduct it. You are not charged for a day the whole factory had off anyway.

11 For HR — adding a person

One record per human. Find it under Human Resources → Employee Roster.

1

Press "Add" and fill in the person

Name, department, shift, phone, address — one line each. Two boxes deserve extra care, and both are on this screen:

🆔 SAP number — one number, one person

The app refuses two people with the same SAP number. That is on purpose: a duplicate would quietly split one person's leave history in two, and nothing would look broken while it happened.

📅 Date joined — drives the fair share

Somebody who joins in July should not get a whole year's leave. The app works out their fair share from this date. Get it wrong and their first balance is wrong. It is fixable later — but get it right now.

2

Give them a login — one click, if they need one

Press "Create login" on their row. Not everybody needs one. A record without a login is still a full member of staff with full leave — HR can always request leave on their behalf. Removing a login later never removes the person or their history.

🗑️
Never delete a person

When someone leaves the company, mark them inactive on the roster instead of deleting them. Their leave history must survive — it is the company's record, and payroll may need it. The app guards against this, but the habit matters more.

👑
About Super Admin

Only the system owner can make a Super Admin. HR cannot see that option, cannot grant it, and the system itself refuses it. That is deliberate — no matter what, "god mode" stays with the owner.

13 For approvers — your queue

If you can sign leave requests, the menu shows Leave Approvals. Open it and the app has already sorted your work.

🎯 Waiting for you

Requests sitting at your stage right now. These are the ones with buttons.

👀 Waiting on others

In the pipeline, but not at your stage yet. You can look. The chain has not reached you, so there is nothing to press.

🗄️ Decided

Finished requests, each wearing its own outcome tag: green Approved, red Rejected with the written reason, grey Cancelled. Newest decision first.

Two buttons, two honest rules

✓

Approving

Press "Sign as …". Your signature moves the request to the next stage — and only ever the next stage. Nobody, not even the system owner, can skip ahead or sign two stages with one press.

✕

Rejecting

Write a reason first — the app insists, because the employee is going to read your words. Write something clear and kind: "cover is too thin that week; try the following week" helps far more than "rejected".

✂
HR can cancel, and reverse

HR and the system owner can cancel any pending request, and reverse an approved one — with a written reason recorded on the request, because reversing signed-off paid leave must be explainable later. A card for someone who has left the company says so plainly, so nobody signs a leaver’s leave by accident.

🤝
Stepping in, honestly recorded

If an approver is away, the Head of HR can sign at their stage instead — or a named deputy can. The record always says who really signed and for whom. Nothing is faked.

14 For HR — reports & the printed form

Two tools for the questions management actually asks.

📊 The Leave Report

Human Resources → Leave Report. Every person, their days — entitled, taken, left. Who is off today. And when a department has too many people away at once, it warns you before cover becomes a problem.

🖨️ The printed form

Press the printer button on any request. Out comes the official form — black on white, all three signatures, dates and reasons filled in — ready for the cabinet. It always matches the app, because the app wrote it.

🧾
Year-end, without the headache

Every balance in the report is worked out from the request history itself — there is no separate tally that can drift out of step. When HR wants the year-end picture, it is already on this screen.

15 Settings HR can change

Every rule in this guide is a setting, not a law of nature. Find them under Administration → System Config → 📅 Leave Policy.

SettingWhat it decides
Rest daysWhich days of the week are never deducted. Sunday only, or Saturday too — tick what the factory really does.
Public holidaysThe days the whole country has off. Update them each January when the government announces them.
Leave daysHow many annual and casual days a year. 21 is the current setting; the app holds whatever HR sets.
Handover notesFrom how many days a handover note becomes required. Currently 3 working days.
Leave yearWhen the leave year starts. January now; a July-to-June cycle is one setting away.
Approval stages & deputiesWho signs, in which order, and who steps in when an approver is away.
Rejection reasonsKeep this on. Employees deserve to know why.
⚡
Change a setting, not the software

None of these need a developer or a rebuild. Change it, press save, and it applies from that moment. Old requests keep the numbers they were approved with — history stays honest.
