🌍The Big Picture — What Is Going On Here?

The whole report squeezed into five friendly sentences.

EKOFIN is a Nigerian food company based in Epe, Lagos — your own neighbourhood. It runs two businesses under one roof:

  • 🌾 Agric: It buys raw cashew nuts from farmers across six Nigerian states, processes them in its Epe factory, and ships the finished kernels to big buyers in Europe and Asia.
  • 🥫 FMCG (Fast-Moving Consumer Goods — everyday supermarket items like seasonings, drink powders and bottled water): It manufactures and packages food products for other brands, and also owns some of its own mini-brands.

In late 2025, something huge happened: construction giant Julius Berger handed over the keys to a modern 60-tonne-per-day cashew factory in Epe on a long lease. EKOFIN suddenly has industrial muscle — but muscle without a nervous system is just weight. And that's where you come in.

The report's conclusion: EKOFIN likely runs on paper, WhatsApp messages and Excel sheets. No proper company-wide software. Your job opportunity is to become the person who builds that missing nervous system — connecting farm ➡️ factory ➡️ ship ➡️ customer ➡️ boss's phone, all in real time.

🧠 In plain English: Imagine a growing restaurant chain where every order is written in notebooks and the owner finds out sales by phoning each branch. EKOFIN is that restaurant — but for cashews and food packets, selling to the world. You'd be the one installing the till system, the kitchen screens and the owner's dashboard.

💰The Two Money Machines

"Two Divisions, One Mission" — here's what each one actually does, step by step.

🌾 Division 1: AGRIC (Cashew Export)

Africa's Farms → Global Markets
  • Sourcing: buys raw cashews straight from farmers and cooperatives across six states in Nigeria's "cashew belt" through six sourcing hubs
  • Cleaning & shelling: removes the outer shell to extract the kernel (the tasty part we eat)
  • Grading: sorts kernels by size and whiteness into export grades — buyers abroad pay different prices per grade
  • Packaging: seals kernels for export containers
  • Byproducts: even the waste makes money — shells yield CNSL (Cashew Nut Shell Liquid, an industrial oil used in paints, brakes and resins) and the leftover shell mass becomes biomass fuel 🔥
  • Selling: bulk contracts to international commodity buyers (mostly Europe, some Asia), earning foreign currency 💵

🥫 Division 2: FMCG (Contract Manufacturing)

Global Foods → African Homes
  • Contract manufacturing: other brands bring recipes; EKOFIN produces on their behalf (co-packing)
  • Formulation & blending: mixing ingredients — seasonings, beverage powders, non-dairy creamers
  • Packaging: filling and sealing retail-ready packs
  • Private label: making products sold under the client's brand name (the shop's own label)
  • Product development: helping brands create new food products from scratch
  • NAFDAC support: guiding clients through Nigeria's product-registration process
  • Own mini-brands ("ready-made brands"): 🚰 Hotelux (bottled water + roasted cashews, popular with hotels) and 🍫 Creviva (creamers, chocolate drink mixes, seasonings) — available for other businesses to rebrand and sell

🧮 How to read “KPI” targets like a pro

A KPI (Key Performance Indicator) is just a scoreboard number agreed BEFORE the project starts, so nobody argues afterwards about whether it worked. Example: today, getting an order from buyer to shipped goods might take 10 days of phone calls and signatures. Promise the ERP will cut it to ~7 days (−30%), measure it monthly, show the graph. Numbers end debates. 📉😤➡️😌

🧠 In plain English: Never promise "digital transformation". Promise one scoreboard number per project, with a deadline. That's what separates architects from app-hobbyists in the MD's eyes. 🎯
