Dear Sir/Madam,

I am writing to you from your own neighbourhood — I live in Iraye-Oke, Epe, minutes from your facility on Adebowale Road — to offer myself as EKOFIN's Chief Software Architect. My vantage point is unusual and, I believe, exactly what this role demands. I did not learn manufacturing's data problems from slides; I learned them holding a clipboard beside running machines. Every shift I watched critical figures — densities, weights, waste, machine stoppages, pallet movements — vanish into paper notebooks, transcribed late, aggregated later, acted upon rarely. I concluded that the distance between what happens on a factory floor and what leadership knows is, fundamentally, a software problem — and I solved it. The platform covers QC testing, waste intelligence, silo and machine health, pallet logistics, governance with three-tier role-based access, and boardroom-grade reporting, all synchronised in real time and hardened against power and connectivity failure through an offline-first architecture.

Your public commitments to rigorous testing, batch traceability, NAFDAC compliance and international food-safety standards are promises that only systems can keep at scale. Export buyers will increasingly ask not merely what you shipped but demand instant proof — which farm, which batch, which test result. The companies that answer within seconds will take the contracts; the rest will wait.

Imagine your weighing bridges, grading lines, blending halls and packaging bays all speaking one digital language — every kernel weighed, every batch graded streaming to your screen the second it happens, from the farm-gates of six states to the export dock at Epe.

Imagine leadership unchained from distance: watching procurement climb, quality checks pass and containers close toward Rotterdam from Lagos, London or Dubai — while stale booklets and Excel graveyards become relics your competitors still depend on.

Imagine your buyers tracing shipments back to the very farming communities that grew them, verifying certificates through world-class portals that carry the EKOFIN name with pride.
