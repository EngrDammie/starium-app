Starium Rafa ERP — Engineering Blueprint

Resource Planning & Profit Modelling

From raw materials to packaged cartons — how every kilogram of input becomes revenue, profit, and a decision the Plant Manager can trust.

Raw Materials → Powder Powder → Sachets Sachets → Cartons Cost → Profit
01What the Plant Manager Asked For

In plain English: "I put raw materials in. I get detergent powder out. I pack that powder into sachets (85g, 22g, 40g, 850g). I put sachets in cartons. I sell the cartons. Tell me — for every Naira I spend on raw materials, how many Naira do I get back? And how much powder, sachets, and cartons does that make?"

🎯 The Core Question

If I spend ₦X on raw materials, how many kilograms of powder do I make? How many sachets of each size? How many cartons? And what is my revenue, cost, and profit at the end?

02The Whole Journey — Visual Flow

Every factory run follows the same path. The feature makes this path visible, calculable, and editable.

1
📥 Raw Materials In
Surfactants (LABSA, SLES), builders (Soda Ash, Zeolite), enzymes, fragrance, colour, fillers. Each has a cost per kg.
▼
2
⚙️ Standard Recipe (Bill of Materials)
Fixed ratios: e.g., 1000 kg powder = 120 kg LABSA + 80 kg SLES + 300 kg Soda Ash + 5 kg Enzyme + 2 kg Fragrance + 493 kg Filler. Yield % accounts for dust loss, moisture, off-spec.
▼
3
🏭 Powder Produced (kg)
Total finished powder after mixing, drying, sieving. This is the "bridging quantity" — it connects raw material cost to packaging.
▼
4
📦 Packed into Sachets → Cartons
Powder split across SKUs: 85g × N sachets, 22g × M sachets, 40g × P sachets, 850g × Q sachets. Each SKU has pieces-per-carton from Gram Specs.
▼
5
💰 Revenue, Cost, Profit
Selling price per carton × cartons = Revenue. Raw material cost + packaging cost + conversion cost = Total Cost. Revenue − Cost = Profit. Margin % = Profit ÷ Revenue.
03The Mathematics — Formulas a 5-Year-Old Can Follow
Total Raw Material Cost (₦)
For each ingredient i: cost_i = qty_kg_i × price_per_kg_i Total_Raw_Cost = Σ cost_i (sum over all ingredients)
Powder Produced (kg) from a Given Raw Material Batch
Recipe defines: input_kg_per_batch → output_kg_per_batch Yield_Percent = (output_kg_per_batch ÷ input_kg_per_batch) × 100 If you spend ₦Total_Raw_Cost: total_input_kg = Σ qty_kg_i powder_kg = total_input_kg × (Yield_Percent ÷ 100)
How Many Sachets of Each Size?
Packing_Plan defines the split (percentages must total 100%): pct_85g + pct_22g + pct_40g + pct_850g = 100% Powder allocated to each SKU: kg_85g = powder_kg × pct_85g ÷ 100 kg_22g = powder_kg × pct_22g ÷ 100 kg_40g = powder_kg × pct_40g ÷ 100 kg_850g = powder_kg × pct_850g ÷ 100 Sachet counts: sachets_85g = (kg_85g × 1000) ÷ 85 sachets_22g = (kg_22g × 1000) ÷ 22 sachets_40g = (kg_40g × 1000) ÷ 40 sachets_850g = (kg_850g × 1000) ÷ 850
Cartons per SKU (from System Config → Gram Specs)
// Each gram spec already has: piecesPerCarton = bags + freebies cartons_85g = sachets_85g ÷ piecesPerCarton_85g cartons_22g = sachets_22g ÷ piecesPerCarton_22g cartons_40g = sachets_40g ÷ piecesPerCarton_40g cartons_850g = sachets_850g ÷ piecesPerCarton_850g total_cartons = cartons_85g + cartons_22g + cartons_40g + cartons_850g
The Money — What Comes In, What Goes Out, What Stays
// Revenue (selling side) revenue_85g = cartons_85g × selling_price_per_carton_85g revenue_22g = cartons_22g × selling_price_per_carton_22g revenue_40g = cartons_40g × selling_price_per_carton_40g revenue_850g = cartons_850g × selling_price_per_carton_850g Total_Revenue = Σ revenue_sku // Cost (making side) packaging_cost_85g = cartons_85g × carton_cost_85g packaging_cost_22g = cartons_22g × carton_cost_22g packaging_cost_40g = cartons_40g × carton_cost_40g packaging_cost_850g = cartons_850g × carton_cost_850g Total_Packaging_Cost = Σ packaging_cost_sku conversion_cost = powder_kg × conversion_cost_per_kg // electricity, labour, maintenance per kg powder Total_Cost = Total_Raw_Cost + Total_Packaging_Cost + conversion_cost // Profit & Margin Profit = Total_Revenue − Total_Cost Margin_Percent = (Profit ÷ Total_Revenue) × 100 // Key ratios the Plant Manager wants Profit_Per_Naira_Raw_Material = Profit ÷ Total_Raw_Cost Revenue_Per_Naira_Raw_Material = Total_Revenue ÷ Total_Raw_Cost Cartons_Per_Naira_Raw_Material = total_cartons ÷ Total_Raw_Cost

05What the Feature Must Do — Phase by Phase
Phase 1 — Foundations
📋 Recipe & Ingredient Master
Create "Standard Recipes" (BOMs) in System Config. Each recipe = name, version, yield %, list of ingredients with fixed kg per batch. Link ingredients to a master price list (₦/kg, editable). Store multiple recipes (premium, standard, economy).
Phase 2 — Planning Screen
🎯 Resource Planning Dashboard
Input: "I have ₦5,000,000 for raw materials" OR "I want to produce 10,000 kg powder". Auto-calculates everything downstream. Sliders for packing plan split (85g/22g/40g/850g %). Live preview of cartons, revenue, cost, profit.
Phase 3 — What-If Scenarios
🔮 Scenario Comparison
Save multiple plans side-by-side: "If I shift 5% from 85g to 850g, what happens to profit?" "If LABSA price rises 15%, what's the new margin?" "Which SKU mix maximises profit given current prices?"
Phase 4 — Actuals vs Plan
📊 Variance Tracking
After production: connect to actual QC test data (powder density, sachet weights, carton counts from existing modules). Show: planned vs actual yield, planned vs actual sachet count, planned vs actual profit. Flag variances > threshold.
Phase 5 — Executive Reports
📈 Profitability Reports
PDF/Excel export for Plant Manager & Finance: Run summary, SKU profitability table, raw material cost trend, packing efficiency, margin waterfall. Schedule weekly auto-email to stakeholders.

10Implementation Roadmap — What to Build First
1️⃣
Week 1-2: Recipe & Price Master
System Config tab: Recipes (CRUD), Ingredient Prices (editable table), SKU Prices & Carton Costs, Conversion Cost. Firestore writes under config/settings.
2️⃣
Week 3-4: Calculation Engine
Pure TS functions in src/services/resourcePlanning.ts — no Firebase, just math. Input: recipe, prices, packing plan, budget/target. Output: full result object. Unit tests for every formula.
3️⃣
Week 5: Planning Page UI
New route /resource-planning. Left/centre/right layout. Sliders for packing plan. Live recalc on every keystroke (debounced). Save plan button → writes to planning_runs.
4️⃣
Week 6: Scenario Compare
Side-by-side view of 2-3 saved plans. Highlight differences. "What if LABSA +15%?" button that clones plan with overridden price.
5️⃣
Week 7: Actuals & Variance
Link to shift test data. Page to record actuals per run. Variance table (Plan vs Actual) with conditional formatting.
6️⃣
Week 8: Reports & Polish
PDF export (jsPDF), Excel export (SheetJS), scheduled email via Cloudflare Worker. Add to Reports menu. Role guards. End-to-end test.
11Key Decisions to Confirm with Plant Manager
❓
Recipe Count
How many standard recipes? (Premium, Standard, Economy, Export?) Start with ONE.
❓
Price Source
Who updates ingredient prices? Finance? Procurement? How often? (Monthly? Per PO?)
❓
Selling Prices
Are carton selling prices fixed per SKU or do they vary by customer/region?
❓
Packing Plan
Is the % split fixed per recipe or does the Plant Manager decide per run?
❓
Conversion Cost
What goes into ₦/kg conversion? (Power, labour, maintenance, depreciation?) Single number or breakdown?
❓
Actuals Source
Which existing module has the "real" powder kg produced? QC Density? Packing records?
12The "5-Year-Old" Summary
🧸 Imagine This...

You have a magic recipe book. You tell it: "I have ₦5 million to buy ingredients." The book instantly tells you:

📦 "You'll make 10,000 kg of powder."
🧴 "That becomes 21 million sachets — mostly 85g and 22g."
📦 "Packed into 81,645 cartons."
💰 "You'll sell them for ₦316 million."
💸 "It costs you ₦44.9 million to make."
💚 "You keep ₦271 million profit — that's 86% margin."
📈 "For every ₦1 you spend on raw materials, you get ₦7.20 profit back."

Then you say: "What if I make more 850g and fewer 22g?" — and the book recalculates in a blink.

That's the feature. Simple inputs. Instant, trustworthy answers. No spreadsheets. No guesswork.
